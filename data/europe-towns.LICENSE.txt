European settlement catalogue
=============================

Source: GeoNames cities500 daily gazetteer extract
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities500.zip
https://www.geonames.org/
Source documentation: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Credit: GeoNames contributors.

Changes made by Arcane Path:
- Retained populated places (PPL, PPLA, PPLA2, PPLA3, PPLA4, PPLC)
  with recorded population at least 2,000.
- Filtered using the game's existing operational European country/coordinate bounds.
- Kept GeoNames ID, name, latitude, longitude, country and population.
- Sorted records, serialized as JSON and gzip-compressed.

The envelope in europe-towns.json.gz records retrieval time and source/license.
Rebuild with: .venv/bin/python scripts/prepare_town_catalogue.py

Data is supplied as is. Population/administrative classifications are not
equivalent to OSM's city/town tags. No road or pedestrian connectivity is implied.
